# Limits of exact historical replay

These limits distinguish unavailable historical inputs from methods that can be rerun on the deposited data. A reconstructed result is not an original author checkpoint or an experimental measurement.

## Cannabis

- The final-paper cohort mapping is incomplete: the bundled source has 3171 labelled records and 2749 query records; the article's final prepared cohort differs.
- The complete final CCD occurrence/primary-metabolite annotation ledger and original candidate component predictions are unavailable. Unknown annotation is not treated as a negative annotation.
- Original Syntelly data and weights are unavailable. The public reconstruction follows the documented 2024 method with declared data, feature and search-budget differences.
- New DMPNN/RTE fits and paired validation provide new computed evidence. They do not recover unpublished author checkpoints, exact historical folds or the custom fragment dictionary.

## Heracleum

- There are 65 master-versus-original-structure conflicts. Published-snapshot reproduction retains them; strict interpretation withholds their ranks/names.
- Table S4 can be compared by record-associated original structures or by master prediction structures. These different identity definitions give different agreement values.
- The published Pabularinone all-eight-endpoint claim disagrees with the deposited values under the stated median rule. Source labels and thresholds are preserved.
- Original prepared docking inputs and raw active/inactive control scores are missing. Published pose observations can be reaggregated; new public-structure docking is a declared reconstruction.
- Original SynMap coordinates are unavailable; new projections are not labelled as recovered original coordinates.

## Mannich

- The curated records and structures are available. Manual literature extraction is outside this server's scope.
- The catalyst scaffold count is 207 under the implemented pinned rules, versus the reported 204. No cohort is silently changed to force agreement.
- Exact Figure S4 reaction-fingerprint/PCA settings or coordinates are unavailable. The implemented reaction-space analysis is explicitly a reconstruction.
- Ambiguous axial assignments and geometry disagreements remain visible; a generated conformer does not establish experimental stereochemical identity.
